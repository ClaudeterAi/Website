About Claudeter

What is Claudeter?

Claudeter is a lean, AI-native product studio. We don't pad timelines, hire armies of consultants, or resell off-the-shelf tools with a markup.

What does Claudeter build?

From customer calls to back-office workflows: we build AI that handles the repetitive, the complex, and the time-consuming. Voice agents, automation systems, and intelligent products that go live in weeks, not quarters.

Which industries does Claudeter work in?

Claudeter builds for Healthcare & RCM, Fintech & Payments, Enterprise SaaS, Real Estate & PropTech, EdTech & HR and Legal & Compliance.

Where does Claudeter work?

We operate across three markets (USA, UAE, and India), which means we understand the regulatory, cultural, and technical nuances that generic agencies miss.

How big is the Claudeter team?

Our team is small by choice. Everyone who works on your project ships code. No account managers in the loop, no offshore handoffs without oversight.

Process and pricing

How long does a project take?

About six weeks on average. A three-day discovery sprint maps the problem, defines scope and ships a proof of concept, then one week of architecture and design, three to four weeks of build with working features every 48 hours, then launch and handoff.

What is the discovery sprint?

We map your problem space, define scope, and ship a proof-of-concept before you sign anything long-term.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on scope, and Claudeter does not publish fixed prices. Scope is defined in the three-day discovery sprint, which ships a proof of concept before you sign anything long-term.

Do I have to commit to a retainer or long contract?

Every engagement starts with a problem statement, not a retainer. You see a proof of concept before contract.

Ownership and compliance

Who owns what Claudeter builds?

You do. Full IP transfer, always, with no lock-in clauses. Every model and every prompt is documented.

How does Claudeter handle compliance?

Compliance is a feature. HIPAA, DHA, GDPR built in.

Will we understand how the system works?

No black boxes. Every model, every prompt, documented.
