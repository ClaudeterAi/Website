How a Claudeter voice agent works

We build real-time voice bots and call center AI with sub-500ms latency, connected to your CRM, ERP and third-party platforms so every call outcome lands where your team works.

We choose the stack per project rather than resell one platform. Voice and speech work typically draws on ElevenLabs, Deepgram, Retell AI, Twilio and LiveKit, with the language model and integrations picked to fit your call flows, data and compliance needs.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build an AI voice agent?

About six weeks on average. A three-day discovery sprint maps the problem, defines scope and ships a proof of concept, then one week of architecture and design, three to four weeks of build with working features every 48 hours, then launch and handoff.

How much does a custom AI voice agent cost?

Pricing depends on scope, and Claudeter does not publish fixed prices. Scope is defined in the three-day discovery sprint, which ships a proof of concept before you sign anything long-term.

Who owns the voice agent once it is built?

You do. Every engagement ends with full IP transfer: code, prompts and models documented, with no lock-in clauses and no dependency on Claudeter.

Can the agent work with our phone system and CRM?

Yes. Our voice stack includes Twilio and LiveKit, and we connect to CRMs, ERPs, payment gateways, data warehouses and third-party platforms over REST, GraphQL or webhooks.

Is an AI voice agent HIPAA compliant?

Healthcare voice agents are HIPAA-ready and built for UAE DHA compliance from the ground up. Claudeter treats compliance as a feature: HIPAA, DHA and GDPR built in.
